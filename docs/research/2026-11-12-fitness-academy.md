## Programs, photographs, and video

- The project catalogue already includes Pilates, Football, and Personal Training. Catalogue copy is project content; it should not be mistaken for an announcement of a current session.
- The official social feeds show Pilates Matwork/Reformer training, a football-coach curriculum announcement, and promotional/video content. No source reviewed confirmed current openings, price lists, a full qualification framework, named instructors, or future dates after the above announcements.
- The local Pilates gallery (`frontend/public/pics/Pilates_images/`) is documented in the project rights manifest as owner-authorized, with supporting records held outside the repository. The per-file source-post mapping is still missing.
- The local football and personal-training images are identified in the rights manifest as Unsplash/illustrative stock, without exact source/license records. They must not be presented as Defenders historical photographs.
- Two local MP4 files have no origin, permission record, or source-post URL in the manifest; they should not be treated as verified academy footage. Prefer source-linked official posts until provenance is recorded.

- Contact and footer information are shared with the broader Defenders site; the organization-specific contact owner should confirm whether all displayed contact channels are correct for Fitness Academy.

## Recommended page direction

Use an energetic, sports-focused editorial layout, visibly distinct from both security organizations. Give Pilates, football, and personal coaching separate program pathways; show only verified academy media; and build a dated internal timeline that distinguishes **announcement**, **course date**, **retrospective**, and **confirmed completion**. Add a testimonial area only when a genuine review, attribution permission, and any image permission are documented. Do not promise overseas placement or repeat certification/approval claims until their scope and evidence are confirmed.
