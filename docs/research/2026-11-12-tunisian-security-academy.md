## Recommended page direction

Make this an education-first vocational academy page, separate from a security-service company and a sports academy: show the official TSA identity, clear course pathways, prerequisites and certificates only when verified, and learning/training history in a chronological internal archive. Include safe, consent-cleared classroom and practical-training media. Use distinct course cards and an academy-specific contact panel. Present course notices as past announcements until completion and dates are verified; never promise employment or internationally recognized certificates based only on promotional copy.
